# AI Employee Platform --- UI/UX Design Brief

## Design Goal

The platform should feel like a professional business operations
product, not a developer console.

Business owners manage an AI employee. Internal AI trainers manage the
complexity.

## Two Interfaces

### Business Owner

Show: - Dashboard - AI Employee - Calls - Leads - WhatsApp -
Appointments - Business Information - Analytics

Hide technical concepts such as prompts, embeddings, vector databases,
and model-provider configuration.

### Internal AI Trainer

Show: - Businesses - AI Employees - Knowledge - Business Data -
Training/Configuration - Test Lab - Conversations - Knowledge Gaps -
Analytics - Versions - Deployments

## Visual Direction

-   Clean
-   Modern
-   Professional
-   Minimal
-   Trustworthy
-   Business/SaaS oriented

## Product Table

Columns: - Product - Variant - Price - Stock - Status

## Product Details

Show: - Product - Price - Stock - Variant - Status - Scheduled Changes

Actions: - Edit - Schedule Change - Update Stock

## Scheduled Change

``` text
Current Price: ₹15,000
New Price: ₹17,000
Effective Date: 29 Aug 2026
Effective Time: 2:00 PM
[Cancel] [Schedule Change]
```

## Test Lab

Input: "What is the iPhone 15 price?"

Show: - Expected answer - Actual AI answer - Data source - Pass/Fail

## Knowledge Gap

Show: - Customer question - AI response - Reason - Add Information -
Ignore

## Conversation Review

Show: - Customer - AI - Timestamp - Language - Transcript - Retrieved
data - Tool calls - Outcome - Evaluation score

## Responsive

Business dashboard should work on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Trainer
console can prioritize desktop.

## UX Principle

> Business owners should feel they are managing an employee, not
> programming an AI.
